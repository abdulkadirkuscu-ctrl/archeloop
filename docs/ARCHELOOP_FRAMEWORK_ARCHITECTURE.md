# ArcheLoop Framework Architecture v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical architecture of the ArcheLoop framework.

It serves as the single source of truth for:

- Assessment design
- Scoring philosophy
- Personalised reports
- Website content
- Educational content
- Product development
- Future platform development

All future development should remain consistent with the principles defined in this document.

---

# 1. Core Philosophy

ArcheLoop is an integrative archetypal self-awareness framework.

Its purpose is to help people:

**Understand → Interrupt → Integrate**

The framework is built upon one central principle:

> **Awareness is the beginning of integration.**

The goal of ArcheLoop is not perfection.

The goal is increasing integration.

People are not their Shadow Loops.

Shadow Loops are protective adaptations that develop to maintain psychological safety.

Integration gradually restores healthy psychological capacities.

---

Assessment & Scoring

Developmental Capacities are the primary unit of psychological measurement within ArcheLoop.

The Assessment does not measure Shadow Loops directly.

# 3. Developmental Domains

The Assessment measures Developmental Capacities and the protective patterns associated with them.

From these measurements ArcheLoop derives:

- Healthy Capacity Scores
- Shadow Loop Scores
- Archetypal Availability
- Primary Shadow Loop
- Secondary Shadow Loop
- Developmental Imbalance
- Growth Edge
- Integration Direction

---

# 4. Healthy Capacities

Each Developmental Domain contains three Healthy Capacities.

Healthy Capacities represent integrated psychological functioning within each Developmental Domain.

Rather than describing perfection, they describe the healthy developmental direction of each Archetype.

| Archetype | Healthy Capacities |
|------------|-------------------|
| Sovereign | Healthy Visibility • Authentic Leadership • Purposeful Action |
| Magician | Authentic Expression • Self-Honesty • Clear Thinking |
| Lover | Emotional Openness • Genuine Connection • Emotional Flow |
| Warrior | Self-Respect • Connected Strength • Inner Vitality |

Healthy Capacities represent developmental direction rather than perfection.

---

## Assessment Measurement Model

Each Developmental Capacity is measured through five questions.

Two Healthy questions measure the availability of the capacity.

Three Shadow questions measure the protective process that develops when the capacity becomes disrupted.

Every Developmental Capacity contains:

### Healthy Capacity

Measures the internal psychological ability.

### Healthy Expression

Measures how consistently that capacity is expressed in everyday life.

### Protective Belief

Measures the underlying belief maintaining the Shadow Loop.

### Protective Emotion

Measures the emotional experience associated with the Shadow Loop.

### Protective Behaviour

Measures the behaviour protecting the individual from perceived psychological threat.

Together these five questions provide a balanced picture of both healthy functioning and protective adaptation within each Developmental Capacity.
---

# 5. Shadow Loops

Each developmental domain contains three Shadow Loops.

Each Shadow Loop represents a protective adaptation that develops when healthy functioning becomes unavailable.

Every Shadow Loop belongs to one Protective Formation.

| Archetype | Collapse | Compensate | Collide |
|------------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Sovereign | Dimmed Light | Paper Crown | Stalled Flame |
| Magician | Blank Page | Smoky Mirrors | Mind Maze |
| Lover | Emotional Lockdown | Fantasy Fog | Flooded Waters |
| Warrior | Compliance | Fortress | Barren Ground |

This twelve-loop architecture is fixed throughout ArcheLoop.

---

# 6. Protective Formations

Every Shadow Loop develops through one of three Protective Formations.

## Collapse

Healthy expression becomes psychologically unavailable.

Authentic expression withdraws.

Collapse answers:

> **What becomes unavailable?**

---

## Compensate

Healthy expression no longer feels reliable or safe.

The psyche produces a protective imitation.

Artificial expression replaces authentic expression.

Compensate answers:

> **What becomes performative?**

---

## Collide

Multiple psychological systems become active simultaneously but cannot work together.

Authentic expression becomes trapped in internal conflict.

Collide answers:

> **What becomes psychologically conflicted?**

---

# 7. Structural Dynamics

The Assessment identifies the visible developmental pattern.

The Framework explains how that pattern developed.

Classification determines what the Assessment measures.

Structural Dynamics explain why that pattern developed.

Every Shadow Loop therefore contains explanatory information beyond its visible presentation.

## Injured Archetype

The parent Archetype whose healthy developmental capacity has become psychologically unavailable, disrupted or destabilised.

The Injured Archetype determines loop ownership.

Examples:

- Paper Crown → Sovereign
- Smoky Mirrors → Magician
- Flooded Waters → Lover
- Fortress → Warrior

---

## Compensating Archetype

Some Shadow Loops recruit another Archetype protectively.

The Compensating Archetype explains the protective strategy.

It does **not** determine loop ownership.

Examples:

Paper Crown

Injured Archetype:

Sovereign

Compensating Archetype:

Magician

The visible pattern remains Sovereign.

The protective strategy is Magician.

# 8. Assessment Measurement Model

Healthy Capacities and Shadow Loops are measured differently.

## Healthy Capacities

Each Healthy Capacity contains two questions.

### Internal Capacity

Measures psychological ability.

### Behavioural Expression

Measures how that ability appears in everyday life.

---

## Shadow Loops

Each Shadow Loop contains three questions.

### Cognitive

Measures the underlying belief.

### Emotional

Measures the emotional experience.

### Behavioural

Measures the protective behaviour.

Together these describe one coherent psychological sequence.

Belief

↓

Emotion

↓

Protective Behaviour

This sequence reflects how protective patterns are understood within ArcheLoop.

---

# 9. Scoring Philosophy

The assessment does not diagnose personality.

It identifies the person's current developmental pattern.

The assessment measures:

- Healthy Capacities
- Shadow Loop activation
- Developmental imbalance
- Relative Archetypal availability

The Assessment identifies the most active visible Shadow Loop.

The Framework then explains the underlying protective dynamics responsible for its development.

Therefore:

> **The Assessment measures the visible developmental pattern.**

> **The Framework explains the underlying protective dynamics that created it.**

---

# 10. Integration Philosophy

Integration does not remove Shadow Loops.

Integration increases access to Healthy Capacities.

As Healthy Capacities strengthen:

- Collapse decreases.
- Compensate becomes unnecessary.
- Collide gradually resolves.

Integration therefore represents increasing psychological flexibility rather than eliminating protective adaptations.

---

# 11. Framework Principles

- Every person contains all four Archetypes.
- Every Archetype can express Healthy, Collapse, Compensate and Collide patterns.
- Shadow Loops are protective adaptations, not identities.
- Behaviour is described rather than judged.
- Development is prioritised over perfection.
- ArcheLoop is educational rather than diagnostic.
- The goal of the Assessment is awareness.
- The goal of Integration is greater psychological flexibility.

---

# 12. Design Principles

The ArcheLoop framework is intentionally designed around a balanced developmental architecture.

Its design follows these principles:

- Four Archetypes represent four fundamental developmental domains.
- Each Archetype contains three developmental capacities.
- Each developmental capacity can fail through one Protective Formation:
  - Collapse
  - Compensate
  - Collide
- This creates twelve Shadow Loops.
- The Assessment contains sixty questions:
  The Assessment contains twelve Developmental Capacities.

Each Developmental Capacity is measured through five questions:

- Healthy Capacity
- Healthy Expression
- Protective Belief
- Protective Emotion
- Protective Behaviour

This creates a balanced sixty-question assessment.

The Assessment measures Developmental Capacities.

Shadow Loops are derived from those measurements.

Reports explain the underlying protective dynamics rather than simply presenting scores.
- Reports explain mechanisms rather than simply presenting scores.
- Integration focuses on strengthening Healthy Capacities rather than fighting Shadow Loops.
- The Assessment measures the visible developmental pattern.
- The Report explains the underlying protective dynamics that created it.
